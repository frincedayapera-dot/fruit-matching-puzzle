Privacy Policy

App: Fruit Matching Puzzle (package: com.frinova.fruitmatchingpuzzle)
Developer: Frinova
Effective date: November 16, 2026

Fruit Matching Puzzle is a fruit memory-matching game. This policy explains what information the app stores, what it sends, and who receives it. We keep data collection to the minimum the game needs.

1. Summary

  • The game has no accounts, no login, no ads, and no analytics or tracking.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The only time information leaves your device is when you choose to send Feedback (see section 3).
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or advertising ID.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue playing, the app saves the following on your device (using the device's local app storage, or your browser's local storage in the web version):

  • Your player name (a nickname you type; the default is "Player")
  • Game progress: stars, level scores, coins, power-ups, unlocked card themes and profile pictures, daily reward dates, and Endless Rush records
  • Your settings: sound, music, volume, and selected music track

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app, clear the app's storage, or clear your browser data. All in-game purchases use coins earned by playing; the game does not sell anything for real money.

3. Information you choose to send: Feedback

The Feedback screen lets you send a message to the developer. When you press Send, the app sends:

  • The message you typed and the type you selected (Feedback or Suggestion)
  • Your player name
  • The app version

The message is delivered by the form service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards it by email to the developer. If sending is not possible, the app may open your email app so you can send the message yourself; in that case your email provider and your email address are involved, as in any email.

Please do not include personal information (such as your full name, address, phone number, or school) in your feedback. We use feedback only to fix bugs and improve the game, and we do not sell it or share it for advertising.

4. Third parties

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): a form-to-email service that delivers your optional feedback to the developer.
  • Google Play: distributes the Android app and may collect information under Google's own privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the web version of the game; GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses under its own privacy policy.

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Children's privacy

The game is suitable for children and does not ask for an account or any personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Feedback is optional, and children should not enter personal details in it. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you remove it by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. Feedback emails are kept only as long as needed to handle the report. To ask us to delete a feedback message you sent, email us at the address below.

7. Security

Feedback is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date above.
