Fruit Matching Puzzle is a colorful memory game for kids and the whole family!

HOW TO PLAY
- Cards are hidden and shuffled for 3 seconds, then the game begins.
- Flip two cards. Find the matching fruits before time runs out.
- Chain matches for COMBO bonus points.
- Finish a level to earn up to 3 stars.

FEATURES
- 20 levels from easy to expert
- Endless Rush: start with 2 minutes, get +50 seconds for every round you finish
- Earn coins and buy power-ups (Peek, Extra Time, Hint)
- Unlock card colors and themes in the Shop
- Collect stars to unlock fruit profile pictures
- Daily rewards
- Cute cartoon fruits, fun sounds and music
- Works offline

Send your suggestions through the Feedback button inside the game. Have fun!
